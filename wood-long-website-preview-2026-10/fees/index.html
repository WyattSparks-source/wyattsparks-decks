Plain answers about cost

Fees

Cost is one of the first things people ask about, so here is how it works with us.

The consultation

Consultations last 90 minutes. There is a consultation fee, and if you hire us it is credited toward your legal fees. By the end of the consultation you will have a realistic estimate for your case.

How we bill once you hire us

Most family law cases are billed by the hour against a retainer, an advance deposit held in trust and drawn down as work is done. Your engagement agreement sets out the retainer amount, the hourly rates of the attorneys and staff working on your case, and how often you will receive itemized statements.

What drives the cost

  • Agreement vs. dispute. A dissolution where you already agree on everything costs far less than a contested divorce.
  • Children. Custody disputes often involve a guardian ad litem, whose fees are usually shared by the parents.
  • Finances. Businesses, pensions and real estate may need appraisals or valuations.
  • Trial. Preparing for and going to trial is the single largest cost driver.

Common questions

Is there a fee for the initial consultation?

Yes. If you hire Wood & Long, the consultation fee is credited toward your legal fees.

How much does a divorce cost in Ohio?

It depends mostly on how much the spouses disagree. An uncontested dissolution with a complete agreement usually costs far less than a contested divorce with custody disputes, business valuations or a trial. During your consultation we will give you a realistic estimate for your situation.

What makes a family law case more expensive?

The main drivers are the number of disputed issues, how willing each side is to negotiate, the need for experts such as appraisers or custody evaluators, discovery of financial records, and whether the case goes to trial. Reaching agreement on some issues early usually lowers the overall cost.
