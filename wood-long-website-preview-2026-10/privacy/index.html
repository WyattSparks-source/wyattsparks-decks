Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Wood & Long, LLC collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, phone, email, the other party’s name, and anything you write in the consultation form or an email.
  • Basic usage data: pages visited and general device and browser information, collected through privacy-respecting analytics.

How we use it

We use what you send to respond to you, check for conflicts of interest, and schedule a consultation. We use usage data only to improve the website. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use advertising trackers on this site.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the website and our intake process, and only as needed to provide those services, or where the law requires it.

Confidentiality

Information sent before we agree to represent you is not protected in the same way as communications with a client. Please share only what is needed for us to check for conflicts and contact you.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@woodlongfamilylaw.com or (614) 567-3031.
