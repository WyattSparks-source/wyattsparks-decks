Divorce & family law only

Practice areas

Every case we take is a family law case. Start with the situation closest to yours.

Ending a marriage

Divorce

How divorce works in Ohio: grounds, the six-month residency rule, contested and uncontested cases, temporary orders and the steps in Franklin County.

Dissolution

How dissolution of marriage works in Ohio: the separation agreement, the 30-to-90-day hearing, how it differs from divorce and how to convert between them.

Property Division

How Ohio courts divide property in a divorce: marital vs. separate property, equal and equitable division, retirement accounts, QDROs, debts and the home.

Spousal Support

How spousal support works in Ohio: the ORC 3105.18 factors, why there is no statewide formula, how long support lasts, temporary support and modification.

Children and parentage

Child Custody

How Ohio courts allocate parental rights and responsibilities, what shared parenting means, and how custody works for married and unmarried parents.

Child Support

How Ohio calculates child support under ORC Chapter 3119, when a court can deviate, how long support lasts, and how to change an existing order.

Paternity

How paternity is established in Ohio under ORC Chapter 3111, through an acknowledgment, genetic testing, the CSEA or juvenile court, and what it means.

Grandparent Rights

When Ohio grandparents can ask a court for visitation or custody under ORC 3109.051, 3109.11 and 3109.12, and how Troxel v. Granville limits those rights.

Stepparent Adoption

How stepparent adoption works in Ohio probate court under ORC Chapter 3107, when the other parent's consent is required, and what adoption changes.

Safety and protection

Protection Orders

How civil protection orders work in Ohio: same-day ex parte orders, the full hearing, what an order can include, and help for petitioners and respondents.

Agreements

Prenuptial Agreements

Prenuptial and postnuptial agreements in Ohio: what they can cover, what makes them enforceable, why timing and disclosure matter, and how postnups work.

After the decree, and other help

Post-Decree Modification

How to change custody, child support or spousal support after an Ohio divorce or custody order, and how to enforce orders that are not being followed.

Mediation

How family mediation works in Ohio: confidentiality, court-ordered and private mediation, domestic violence screening, and collaborative divorce.

Guardian ad Litem

What a guardian ad litem does in an Ohio custody case, how to work with one, and your options if you disagree with the GAL's report or recommendation.

LGBTQ+ Family Law

Family law for LGBTQ+ couples and parents in Ohio: divorce, relationships that began before 2015, securing parentage through adoption, and prenups.
