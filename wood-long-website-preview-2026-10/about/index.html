About Wood & Long

Two lawyers who decided in 2014 to do one thing well. Four lawyers now, still doing only that.

Why only family law

Jessica Wood and Chelsea Long both practiced at Edward F. Whipps & Associates before they started Wood & Long in 2014. They built the firm around one kind of case so that everyone here works on divorces, custody and support every day: the statutes, the local court rules, how each magistrate tends to see things, and the small practical details that decide how a case goes.

Attorney, and counselor at law

Most people don’t realize a lawyer has two jobs. The attorney’s job is negotiating with your ex’s lawyer and arguing in court. The counselor’s job happens in private: an honest read on your chances, including the goals that probably aren’t achievable.

“The most effective family law attorneys are those who are candid with you, even when the truth is tough to hear.”

We’ll do both jobs. That means sometimes telling you something you didn’t want to hear, early, before you’ve spent money finding it out the hard way.

Court when it’s worth it

Some lawyers litigate every issue, and that drives up fees fast. We’re ready to go to trial, and we do. But a trial costs money, time and sleep, so we’ll walk through what you’d gain by settling and what you’d gain by fighting, and you decide.
