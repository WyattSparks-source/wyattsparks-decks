About Wood & Long

Wood & Long, LLC is a Columbus, Ohio law firm founded in 2014 by Jessica M. Wood and Chelsea L. Long. The firm practices only divorce and family law, including dissolution, custody, child and spousal support, civil protection orders, adoption, and post-decree matters, for clients across Central Ohio.

Why a family-law-only firm

Jessica Wood and Chelsea Long met as associates at Edward F. Whipps & Associates and founded Wood & Long in 2014. They built the firm around one practice area so that every attorney and staff member works on family law cases every day: the statutes, the local court rules, the judges’ and magistrates’ expectations, and the practical details that decide how a case goes.

Family law cases are personal. We take time to understand what you want, explain your options plainly, and give you realistic expectations. Where an agreement serves you, we negotiate one, including through mediation or collaborative law. Where it doesn’t, we prepare for court.
