Jessica Wood&Chelsea Long

Jessica & Chelsea

Jessica Wood and Chelsea Long both practiced at Edward F. Whipps & Associates. Jessica had started there as a legal assistant in 2003 and put herself through law school while working full time. Chelsea finished third in her class at Capital University Law School. In 2014 they opened their own firm and decided it would do one thing: family law.

Jessica grew up as the child of divorced parents. Chelsea is certified by the Ohio State Bar Association as a Specialist in Family Relations Law. Both are appointed by courts as guardians ad litem, so they’ve sat in the chair where someone decides what’s best for the kids. They bring that to every client they represent.

“The most effective family law attorneys are those who are candid with you, even when the truth is tough to hear.”

That line comes from our own About page, and we mean it. A lawyer has two jobs. One is to negotiate and to argue in court. The other, the counselor’s job, is to tell you privately what you can realistically get and what you probably can’t. We do both.

What happens when you call

  1. 1

    Call or fill out the form.

    We’ll ask for your name and the other person’s name. That’s only so we can check we haven’t already talked with them. If we have, we can’t talk with you.

  2. 2

    Sit down with us for 90 minutes.

    At our office (there’s a garage under the building), on Zoom, or by phone. You don’t need to bring anything. Please arrange childcare; this conversation is easier without the kids in the room. We’ll explain how Ohio law applies to you, what your options are, and roughly what it will cost.

  3. 3

    Go home and think it over.

    The consultation has a fee. If you hire us, it comes off your bill. If you don’t, that’s fine.
