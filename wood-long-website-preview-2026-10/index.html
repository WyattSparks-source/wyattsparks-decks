Your first step

What happens when you contact us

  1. 1
    You reach out. Call us or send the consultation form. We check for conflicts of interest before we talk about your case.
  2. 2
    We meet for 90 minutes. You tell us what happened and what you want. We explain how Ohio law applies, your realistic options, and what it is likely to cost.
  3. 3
    You decide. There’s no pressure to hire us that day. If you do, your consultation fee is credited toward your fees.
