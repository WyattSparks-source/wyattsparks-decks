What happens when you contact us

  1. You reach out. Call us or send the consultation form. We check for conflicts of interest before we talk about your case.
  2. We meet for 90 minutes. You tell us what happened and what you want. We explain how Ohio law applies, your realistic options, and what it is likely to cost.
  3. You decide. If you hire us, your consultation fee is credited toward your fees and we get to work on a plan.
