Partner · Family Law Attorney

Brandy A. Wyatt

BW

Family law attorney and Ohio Domestic Violence Network panel attorney, handling divorce, custody, grandparent rights and dissolutions across Central Ohio.

Brandy Wyatt handles divorces, custody disputes, grandparent rights cases and dissolutions across Central Ohio, and has particular experience with cases involving domestic violence. She is a panel attorney for the Ohio Domestic Violence Network.

Before law, she was an award-winning newspaper journalist and a Kiplinger Fellow in Public Affairs Journalism at The Ohio State University. She graduated cum laude from Capital University Law School as a night student while working full time as a law clerk, and was a member of the Moot Court National Team and the Order of Barristers.
