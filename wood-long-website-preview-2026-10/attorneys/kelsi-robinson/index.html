Kelsi Robinson

Associate attorney focused on child welfare and family law.

Kelsi Robinson is an associate attorney with a commitment to child welfare and family law. At Capital University Law School she served as Co-Chief Justice of the Moot Court program, competed on the Child Welfare & Adoption Law Moot Court team, and earned the Order of Barristers.

A Columbus native, she earned her bachelor’s degree in American History from The Ohio State University. Before law school she worked as a community health worker and community organizer, and she serves on the board of Butterfly of Hope, a nonprofit providing mental health resources to local students.
