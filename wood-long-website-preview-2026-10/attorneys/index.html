How to choose the right attorney for your case

The right fit depends on your case more than on titles. If you expect a negotiated outcome, look for someone who settles cases well, including through mediation or collaborative law. If the other side is likely to fight, look for someone comfortable in a courtroom. If your case involves domestic violence, ask about experience with civil protection orders.

You should also feel able to talk to your attorney plainly. A good attorney explains your options in plain English and gives you realistic expectations, even when they are not what you hoped to hear. During your consultation, tell us what matters most to you and we will suggest who on our team fits best.
