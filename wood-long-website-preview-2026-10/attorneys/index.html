Not sure who to ask for?

You don’t have to choose. Tell us what’s going on when you call, and we’ll suggest who fits. If you expect to settle, you may want someone who does a lot of negotiating and mediation. If the other side is going to fight, you want someone comfortable in a courtroom. If there’s been violence, ask who handles protection orders. And you should be able to talk to your lawyer without translating. If something doesn’t click, say so.
