Jessica M. Wood

Grew up a child of divorce. Worked her way up from legal assistant.

Talk with JessicaCall (614) 567-3031

Jessica Wood grew up as the child of divorced parents, and it shapes how she practices. She looks hard for an agreement a family can live with, and she prepares for court when the other side won’t get there.

She started at Edward F. Whipps & Associates as a legal assistant in 2003, went through Capital University Law School while working full time, and became an associate there in 2010. In 2014 she and Chelsea Long opened Wood & Long.

She handles dissolutions, contested divorces, custody cases, child and spousal support, and civil protection orders. She is trained in mediation and collaborative family law, serves as a guardian ad litem, and is a panel attorney for the Ohio Domestic Violence Network.

Outside the office

Jessica is married with two sons and a close circle of friends. For more than ten years she has volunteered with the Sexual Assault Response Network of Central Ohio.
