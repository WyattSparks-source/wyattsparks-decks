Partner · Family Law Attorney

Jessica M. Wood

Co-founder of Wood & Long, trained divorce and family mediator and collaborative family law attorney, and Ohio Domestic Violence Network panel attorney.

Jessica Wood co-founded Wood & Long in 2014 after a decade at Edward F. Whipps & Associates, where she started as a legal assistant in 2003 and became an associate attorney in 2010.

As the child of divorced parents, she looks first for workable, amicable solutions and litigates when a case needs it. She is trained as a divorce and family mediator and as a collaborative family law attorney, serves as a guardian ad litem, and is a panel attorney for the Ohio Domestic Violence Network.

She earned her J.D. cum laude from Capital University Law School while working full time, with concentrations in children and family law and dispute resolution. For more than ten years she has volunteered with the Sexual Assault Response Network of Central Ohio.
