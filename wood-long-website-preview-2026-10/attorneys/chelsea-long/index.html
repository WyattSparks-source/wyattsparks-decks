Partner · Family Law Attorney

Chelsea L. Long

CL

Co-founder of Wood & Long and a Certified Specialist in Family Relations Law, certified by the Ohio State Bar Association.

Chelsea Long co-founded Wood & Long in 2014 with Jessica Wood. Her practice is limited to divorce and family law, and she is certified by the Ohio State Bar Association as a specialist in family relations law.

She also serves as a court-appointed guardian ad litem, which gives her a close view of how courts weigh the best interests of children. She has served as President of the Central Ohio Association for Justice and sits on the Columbus Bar Association’s guardian ad litem subcommittee.

Chelsea graduated summa cum laude from Capital University Law School, third in her class, where she served on the Capital University Law Review. Before law school she earned a Bachelor of Music, magna cum laude, from Ohio University. She lives in Bexley with her husband and two sons.
